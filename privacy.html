Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

ClipForge is software that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what it does with your data when you connect a social account.

What ClipForge accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to your channel (youtube.upload) and to read your channel's name (youtube.readonly) so it can show which channel is connected.
  • TikTok, Instagram and Facebook: permission to publish videos to the accounts and Pages you choose, and to read their names.

Where your data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only in a local database on your computer. They are never sent to the ClipForge developer or any third party.
  • Videos and their titles and descriptions are sent only to the platform you publish to, when you press Publish.
  • ClipForge has no server, collects no analytics and sells or shares no data.

Google user data

ClipForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to upload your videos and show your channel name, and is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Disconnect an account in ClipForge's Settings to delete its token from your computer. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google account permissions, or in the settings of TikTok, Instagram or Facebook.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.
